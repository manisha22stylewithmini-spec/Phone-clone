The story in three parts

Point of view, pain, solution

The short version. The full reasoning is in the case study.

Point of view

Handoffs should be free

Content is made on a laptop and published from a phone. Bridging the two should take one scan, not ten steps and a plan.

Pain point

Five stages, two devices

  • The file has to travel to the phone
  • Quality can drop in transit
  • Captions typed on a tiny keyboard
  • Existing fixes need an account, ads or a paid plan
Solution

Scan once. The phone appears.

A phone appears in a realistic frame on the laptop. Click to tap, drag to swipe, type on the keyboard, and drop files straight into the Gallery.

The solution

Designed around four promises

Free forever

No ads, no paid plan, no trial. Running it costs nothing, so there is no reason to charge.

One scan

A QR code starts the connection. A USB cable or pairing code is the backup.

Private

The screen stays on the home network. Nothing passes through a server I run.

Feels like a phone

A real phone frame makes the controls obvious without instructions.

Key decisions

The choices that mattered

Web page plus local helper

A browser cannot control a phone alone, and a cloud service would need ads or fees. Going local solved both.

Phone frame, not a window

Setup, errors and the live screen all appear in one place, on the phone itself.

Drag media straight to Gallery

Removes the file-transfer stage, which was the real pain.

Android only, said clearly

iPhones cannot be controlled this way. I stated the limit instead of faking it.
